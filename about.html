Our Lineage

The Pure Poetry of Mulberry Silk

Gloss Robe Peak was founded to restore authentic, sensory luxury to private loungewear. In a world dominated by synthetic polyester imitations that trap moisture and create friction, pure Mulberry silk remains nature's most extraordinary protein textile.

Our master couturiers hand-tailor every dressing gown using unbroken Grade 6A Mulberry silk filaments woven into heavy 22-momme satin, enclosing all internal seam junctions in French rolled hems at our flagship SoHo atelier.
