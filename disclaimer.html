Craftsmanship & Silk Textile Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Gloss Robe Peak, Manhattan Atelier

1. General Couture Notice

The silk sericulture treatises, momme density calculations, protein dermatology commentary, and couture garment guidelines published across the Gloss Robe Peak digital platforms and at our flagship salon located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, are presented exclusively for general educational, historical, and aesthetic appreciation. While our master couturiers and textile scientists make every reasonable effort to ensure that all published technical guidance reflects verified sericulture physics, Gloss Robe Peak makes no medical guarantees regarding dermatological conditions.

2. Silk Fiber Care & Laundering Protocols

Pure Mulberry silk is an organic protein filament that requires delicate maintenance. While silk is naturally strong, harsh chemical detergents, bleach, hot water washing, and high-heat tumble drying will damage the natural sericin coating and break down fibroin protein chains. Patrons must follow our certified cold hand-washing or professional dry-cleaning protocols to preserve the liquid drape, tensile strength, and optical luster of their garments across decades of wear.

3. Natural Botanical Color Nuances & Lightfastness

Our botanical and eco-certified dye formulations are extracted from natural plant sources including madder root, logwood, and wild rose petals. Because botanical pigments react naturally with ambient lighting conditions and pH levels, subtle tonal shifts over years of exposure to natural sunlight are authentic characteristics of living plant dyes.

4. Bespoke Sizing & Handcrafted Variations

Our digital photography and interactive configurator are calibrated to reflect the luminous champagne rose, dusty plum, and pearl blossom textures of our silk loungewear with high fidelity. However, because each robe is individually hand-cut on the bias and sewn by master tailors, subtle variances of several millimeters in seam drape are natural hallmarks of bespoke couture craft. Patrons are invited to inspect silk swatches and sample robes at our Mercer Street boudoir prior to finalizing bespoke orders.

5. Scientific References & Sericulture Studies

Our editorial journal frequently cites historical textile archives, dermatology journals, and sericulture research papers to provide scientific context for our patrons. These scholarly citations are curated solely to enhance your textile appreciation and couture understanding. Gloss Robe Peak exercises no administrative authority, editorial control, or technical oversight over third-party textile institutes or referenced academic laboratories, and we assume no legal liability for external research publications.

6. Limitation of Atelier Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable statutory law, Gloss Robe Peak, its couturiers, executives, and affiliates shall not be held liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from improper living space laundering, aggressive iron scorches, or abrasive friction damage. Our total aggregate liability concerning any verified commercial dispute or craftsmanship claim shall be strictly limited to the verified purchase price paid by the patron for the specific silk garment.
