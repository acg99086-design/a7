Privacy Policy & Patron Data Stewardship

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Gloss Robe Peak, Manhattan Atelier

1. Introduction & Silk Boudoir Confidentiality

At Gloss Robe Peak, operating our luxury silk loungewear atelier and bespoke fitting boudoir at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, we hold client confidentiality and personal privacy to the highest standard of haute couture craftsmanship. We recognize that commissioning bespoke Mulberry silk robes, scheduling private fitting sessions, or ordering custom bridal loungewear suites involves sharing intimate body measurements and personal styling preferences. This Privacy Policy outlines the exact protocols we employ to collect, process, safeguard, and retain your data across our digital portals and Manhattan salon.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you engage with our loungewear atelier, we collect essential client information including your full name, email address, telephone number, billing records, custom monogramming requests, and bespoke anatomical tailoring dimensions recorded during private fittings. Additionally, when you utilize our interactive silk robe configurator or browse our sericulture research treatises, our web servers record technical telemetry such as IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and referral paths via Google Tag manager. These technical metrics enable our digital artisans to optimize browsing performance and protect transaction security across international luxury markets.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate couture garment manufacturing and commercial operations, including fulfilling custom silk robe commissions, coordinating fitting salon appointments, organizing personalized monogram embroidery, generating formal invoices, and delivering shipment tracking updates. Furthermore, with your explicit consent, we may send private invitations to closed-door silk fabric previews and seasonal loungewear collection unveils. We maintain an absolute policy never to sell, rent, monetize, or publicly distribute your personal information or fitting records to any third-party marketing brokers.

4. Data Storage & Cryptographic Protection Standards

All digital commission archives, client garment drafts, and billing information entrusted to Gloss Robe Peak are stored on private cloud servers shielded by 256-bit AES encryption protocols and strict administrative access controls. Digital payment transactions are handled directly through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant processors, ensuring our atelier never retains raw credit card numbers on local hardware. We perform continuous vulnerability assessments and cybersecurity audits to guarantee that your private information remains completely protected from unauthorized external access.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Access Controls

Regardless of your country of residence, Gloss Robe Peak extends comprehensive data privacy rights aligned with global standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You retain the absolute legal right to inspect the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to profile records, restrict processing activities, or demand the permanent deletion of your client records from our systems. To exercise these statutory privacy rights, simply submit a formal request to our concierge desk at any time.

6. Data Retention Protocols & Archival Integrity

We retain client fitting archives and bespoke pattern registries for as long as your patron profile remains active, enabling seamless repeat orders, seasonal colorway expansions, and longitudinal sizing adjustments without requiring repetitive intake sessions. If you choose to conclude your patronage or request data expungement, all electronic records and custom garment drafts are securely purged and permanently deleted within thirty business days, subject only to mandatory statutory tax and accounting retention requirements mandated by United States commercial law.

7. Atelier Contact for Privacy Governance

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to discuss our client confidentiality practices with our leadership team, please contact our administrative desk. Written correspondence should be addressed directly to Gloss Robe Peak, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically to our concierge staff by calling +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours. Our compliance team will examine your communication with professional care and respond within standard business timelines.
